About Me

My academic background and the interdisciplinary research direction that has shaped my work.

I completed my B.Sc. Engineering in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Gopalganj Science and Technology University, Bangladesh.

Undergraduate Thesis

FarmChat: LLM-RAG-Powered Framework for Real-Time Tomato Leaf Disease Detection and Farmer Assistance

Supervisor: Dr. A.T.M. Saiful Islam
  • Developed an end-to-end artificial-intelligence framework for real-time tomato leaf disease detection using YOLO11.
  • Integrated Large Language Models with Retrieval-Augmented Generation to provide Bangla-English agricultural decision support.
  • Designed an explainable AI-assisted pipeline to improve reliability and interpretability.
  • Developed a prototype chatbot for disease diagnosis, cultivation guidance and treatment recommendations.
  • Evaluated the proposed framework using publicly available plant-disease datasets.
